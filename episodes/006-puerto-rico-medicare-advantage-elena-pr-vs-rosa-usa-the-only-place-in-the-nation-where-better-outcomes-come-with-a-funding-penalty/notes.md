Puerto Rico has the highest Medicare Advantage enrollment in the nation — 96.2% of its Medicare population — and the highest quality ratings, hosting the three largest five-star MA contracts in the United States. Yet its benchmark payment rate is roughly 41% below the national average: $778 a month versus $1,313. Through the story of Elena in Puerto Rico and Rosa in Florida, this episode unpacks the ~$3,400-a-month federal support gap, the outdated fee-for-service formula behind it, and the administrative fix — a benchmark floor tied to the U.S. Virgin Islands — that CMS could adopt without an act of Congress.
