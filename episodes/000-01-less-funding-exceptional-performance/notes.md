Here's the healthcare paradox Washington still cannot explain: Puerto Rico receives 41% less Medicare funding than the states — yet it continues to deliver some of the strongest Medicare Advantage quality results in the nation.

How is that possible? And if Puerto Rico can achieve these results while being funded at such a disadvantage, imagine what it could accomplish if Washington funded it fairly.

That is exactly what The Healthcare Paradox explores. Real data. Real outcomes. Real federal policy — explained in plain English. No Washington jargon. No political spin. Just the numbers, the results, and the facts behind Puerto Rico's healthcare funding disparity.

Less funding. Exceptional performance. An unequal formula.
